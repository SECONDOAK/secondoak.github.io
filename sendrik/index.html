Dispatch

A private, agent-first mail client by Second Oak. It reads incoming customer mail, translates it, and drafts replies for its owner to review and send.

What it does

  • Shows the owner's Gmail or Outlook inbox with a summary and translation of each thread.
  • Drafts a reply in the sender's language, using the business's own knowledge base and store data.
  • Sends a reply only when the owner presses Send. The agent never sends mail on its own.

Who it is for

Dispatch is an internal tool used by Second Oak and the businesses it runs. It is not offered to the public, and sign-in is restricted to its owner.
