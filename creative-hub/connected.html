Brand Vault is installed on your store

Your store … is connected.

Brand Vault is a private workspace your agency sets up for you — it isn't self-service. Your store's orders will appear in your workspace as soon as it's linked. Here's what happens next:

  1. Already have a Brand Vault account? Log in, open your workspace, go to Connect → Shopify, enter this store's domain and click Connect. No second approval needed — we already have the store's permission.
  2. Don't have one yet? Ask your Second Oak contact to set up your workspace and invite you. Mention this store's domain and we'll link it for you.
Log in to Brand Vault → Request access

Nothing is read from your store until it's linked to a workspace. You can remove Brand Vault at any time from your Shopify admin under Apps, which revokes our access immediately. See the Privacy Policy.
