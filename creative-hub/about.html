Brand Vault · by Second Oak AB

All your advertising and sales in one place — with creatives to match.

Brand Vault is a marketing workspace for businesses. It connects the ad platforms and the online store a business already uses, shows what the advertising actually returns in real orders, and generates on-brand ad creatives from the brand's own assets.

What it does

One dashboard for every channel

Google Ads, Meta Ads, Microsoft Advertising and TikTok Ads side by side: spend, impressions, clicks and the conversions each platform reports.

Real orders, not platform claims

Shopify orders are matched to the ads that drove them, so return on ad spend is measured in money that actually reached the store.

Decision-ready metrics

ROAS, MER, break-even by gross margin, customer journeys, and before/after analysis of every budget change.

On-brand creatives

Generate ad images from the brand's own logo, colours and product photos, then launch them to Meta directly from the workspace.

How it works with Google Ads

When a workspace manager connects a Google Ads account, Brand Vault asks for the Google Ads API permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords) through Google's standard sign-in. With it, Brand Vault:

  • lists the Google Ads accounts the signed-in user can access, so they can choose which one to connect;
  • reads campaign, ad group and ad names, statuses, and daily performance — spend, impressions, clicks, conversions — and stores them so the dashboard can show trends over time;
  • lets authorised workspace members pause or resume ads from the dashboard.

Google Ads data is used only to show that account's performance inside the workspace it was connected to. It is not shared with other workspaces, not sold, and not used for advertising of our own. Brand Vault's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the account in Brand Vault or from Google Account permissions.

Who it is for

Brand Vault is used by Second Oak and by the businesses we work with. Access is by invitation: Second Oak sets up a private workspace for each business, and the business decides which of its people can view, edit, or manage connections. Workspaces are fully isolated from one another.
